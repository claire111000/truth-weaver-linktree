《真相編織》是一款以記者視角探查社會案件的懸疑推理遊戲，玩家透過採訪和上網蒐集，將情報整理至線索板，逐步還原案件樣貌。

"Truth Weaver" is a suspenseful mystery-solving game where players step into the shoes of a journalist investigating social cases. By conducting interviews and gathering information online, you will organize clues on a deduction board to piece together the truth behind each case.

六角形團隊在學生時期曾多次合作開發幾款遊戲，其中作品《真相編織》，榮獲 2024 金點新秀年度最佳設計獎等多項大獎肯定。

經歷 4 場實體展覽、累積超過 300 位玩家的熱烈回饋與支持，我們於 2024 年 8 月正式展開商業化開發，並於 2025 年 2 月成立「六角形有限公司」。目前正全力開發《真相編織》，期待帶給大家好玩有趣的推理遊戲。

We are an independent game development team based in Taiwan. Having collaborated on several projects during our university years, our graduation title, Truth Weaver, was honored with the 2024 Best of Young Pin Design Award along with multiple accolades.

Following participation in four offline exhibitions and gathering over 300 pieces of player feedback and support, we officially transitioned into commercial development in August 2024 and incorporated Hexagon Studio in February 2025. We are currently giving our all to the development of Truth Weaver, fully dedicated to delivering an engaging and unforgettable mystery game experience to players worldwide.
